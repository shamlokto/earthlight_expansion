16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single elite humanoid combat mecha, the "Directorate Talos": a heavier, darker, menacing real-robot suit in dark gunmetal grey armour with sharp angular plates, wide spiked pauldrons with crimson slashes, a single glowing red mono-eye under a swept crest horn, a back pack with twin thrusters. It holds a long crimson beam sabre raised in a dynamic fencing stance, the blade a glowing red energy blade with a white-hot core. Full body, three-quarter view, facing left. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole mecha is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted dusky purple background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
