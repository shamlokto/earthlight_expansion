16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, same style and same Security Directorate colours as the reference picture. A single elite heavy fire-support combat mecha, the "Directorate Medusa": a stocky, broad, heavily armoured real-robot suit in dark gunmetal grey with sharp angular plates and crimson slashes, a single glowing red mono-eye in a low armoured head, and a huge boxy multi-tube missile launcher pod mounted over its head and shoulders, its many tubes loaded with red-tipped missiles. In its right hand it swings a shock flail: a heavy spiked mace head on a segmented energy chain, crackling with crimson lightning. Thick legs, a back pack with twin thrusters. Full body, three-quarter view, facing left. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole mecha is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted dusky purple background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
