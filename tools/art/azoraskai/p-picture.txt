16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single colossal heroic flagship, the "Azoras Kai": the ultimate rebuild of the flagship in the reference picture, same lineage but bigger and grander. A long heavy armoured carrier-battlecruiser: a layered, multi-tiered armoured drum hull lined with rows of gold turrets, drawn out at the front into a very long sharp needle prow; two large forward-swept wings, each ending in a big glowing wingtip engine pod and carrying TWO massive twin-barrelled heavy cannons stacked along the wing; a raised crowned command bridge with a golden crown-like spire; a big four-nozzle engine block at the stern with bright cyan-white exhaust glow. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Royal pearl-white armour with rich gold trim and deep crimson red stripes and panels, cyan glass and lights. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette, majestic. The whole ship is centred and fills about 90% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
