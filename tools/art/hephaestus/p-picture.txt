16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, same style as the reference picture. A single colossal unarmed fleet repair ship, the "Hephaestus": a gigantic floating space drydock and mother ship. A long, very wide, boxy industrial hull built as an open repair cradle: two huge parallel armoured hull beams joined by heavy girder frames, with a big open repair bay between them where a damaged small destroyer sits held by docking clamps; several large articulated robot crane arms and welding gantries reach over the bay with orange welding sparks; a hangar door glowing at the bow; radiator fins, fuel tanks and cargo containers along the flanks; a tall control tower bridge at the stern with a big green repair cross emblem; huge slow engine block with soft cyan exhaust. No guns at all. Three-quarter view from above, bow pointing to the lower left. Steel grey and white armour with safety-yellow and black hazard stripes, orange panels, green lights, cyan glass. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette, enormous and heavy. The whole ship is centred and fills about 90% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
