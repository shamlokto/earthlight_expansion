== robot-src.jpg (reference: the old robot picture (js/art/pallas.js before 2026-10-28))
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single slim heroic humanoid ace mecha, the "Pallas" hunter form: real-robot proportions, a V-shaped gold crest over a cyan visor, chest made from a folded jet nose and canopy, shoulder armour made from jet air intakes, a folded forward-swept wing binder pack on its back. It holds a long railgun rifle in its right hand and a glowing cyan-white beam sabre in its left hand. Full body, three-quarter view, dynamic stance facing the lower left. White armour with deep blue panels, magenta-pink accents, gold trim. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. Centred, filling about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.

== jet-src.jpg (reference: the old jet picture)
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single sleek transforming space fighter-bomber, the "Pallas" jet form: a long pointed nose with a bubble canopy, forward-swept wings, twin canted tail fins, side air intakes, and one large heavy torpedo slung under the fuselage. Three-quarter view, nose pointing to the lower left. White armour with deep blue panels, magenta-pink accents, gold trim, cyan canopy. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. Centred, filling about 80% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
