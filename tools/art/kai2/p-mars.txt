16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, in the exact style, palette and rendering of the reference picture. A single humanoid combat mecha, the "Mars Kai": a refit of the Union's basic mass-produced mecha, slimmer and more agile than the reference machine: a rounded visor head with a cyan sensor band, compact chest, small round shoulder armour, a beam rifle held in both hands with a short beam bayonet under the barrel, small back thrusters. Full body, three-quarter view, dynamic ready stance, rifle angled to the lower left. White armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan visor and sensor lights. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole mecha is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
