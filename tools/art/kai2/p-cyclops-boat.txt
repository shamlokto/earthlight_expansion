16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single compact armed space missile boat, the "Cyclops Kai": a short, wide, heavily armoured hull bristling with missile launchers: two big box launcher racks on the flanks with rows of round missile tube hatches, a block of vertical launch cells on the spine, red-tipped missiles visible in the open tubes, a small armoured bridge with an amber-lit visor slit, a cyan sensor eye at the bow, and two chunky engine nozzles at the stern. Three-quarter view from above, bow pointing to the lower left. Dark charcoal and gunmetal armour plates with bold hazard-yellow and amber stripes and chevrons, black-and-yellow hazard markings on the launcher racks, red missile tips, cyan and amber lights. No pink, no magenta, no white armour. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole boat is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
