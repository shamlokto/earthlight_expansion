16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, in the exact style, palette and rendering of the reference picture. A single stocky missile-launcher combat mecha, the "Cyclops Kai": a squat armoured humanoid with a single large round cyan mono-eye in its head, two big box missile pods on its shoulders with rows of missile tubes, a missile launcher on one arm, heavy legs, a backpack. Full body, three-quarter view, braced firing stance facing the lower left. White armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan eye and sensor lights. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole mecha is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
