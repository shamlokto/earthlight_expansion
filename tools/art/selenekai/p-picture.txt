16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single sleek fast space carrier, the "Selene Kai": one wide flat arrowhead-shaped hull like a delta wing with a single sharp bow point (no prongs, no twin spikes), a domed bridge with a cyan canopy and a small beam turret on its spine, two long straight launch rails mounted on the wingtips running parallel to the hull and reaching past the bow, a hangar bay opening under the bow, and a pair of big engine nacelles at the stern with glowing exhaust. Three-quarter view from above, bow pointing to the lower left. Pink and white armour plates with magenta stripes, gold trim on the rails. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette, retro 16-bit pixel art. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
