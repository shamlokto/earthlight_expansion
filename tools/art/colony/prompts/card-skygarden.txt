Sky Garden: lush terraced gardens under the colony's sky panels. Three low stepped garden terraces with off-white stone retaining walls, rows of bright green shrubs, flowering beds with small pink and yellow flowers and two round leafy trees, a little stream of clear blue water trickling down the steps, the whole garden sheltered by one large curved arching glass sky panel on slim white ribs that vaults over it like a half-open canopy, sunlit and airy, on a square plated deck base.
