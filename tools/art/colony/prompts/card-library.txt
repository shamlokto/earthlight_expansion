Great Library, the Archive of Earth: a stepped white stone plinth with a glowing teal band, a ring of slender white columns around a great pale reading dome trimmed with gold and crowned by a small blazing golden lantern, two long wings of warm lit stacks with amber window bands on either side, broad white steps in front flanked by two gold-tipped obelisks. Scholarly, calm and monumental, filling most of the square base.
