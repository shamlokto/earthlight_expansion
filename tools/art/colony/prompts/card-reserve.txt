Emergency Stores: a squat armoured supply bunker of olive-grey steel with a rounded roof and bold yellow-and-black hazard stripes along its edges, a heavy sealed door with a red cross-and-wheat emblem, stacked crates of ration packs, two blue water drums and a rack of white air canisters beside it under a small awning, a green status lamp over the door, on a square base of grey deck plates. Compact and sturdy, filling most of the base.
