Lift: a tall lift tower between the colony's decks. A sturdy open square lattice frame of grey steel girders with diagonal cross-bracing, taller than wide, a boxy elevator cage of metal bars with a warm-lit floor hanging inside the frame, a heavy winch housing with a big cable drum and a small amber warning light on top, thick hoist cables running down. Below the cage, the square base tile is cut open by a deep pitch-black square shaft going down into darkness, its edge framed by a bright gold rim with yellow hazard stripes, on a square plated deck base around it.
