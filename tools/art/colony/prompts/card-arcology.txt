Arcology: a city in one building. A massive stepped pyramid-tower of white and pale-gold terraces rising to a slender spire, every terrace lined with green gardens and small trees, thousands of tiny warm lit windows, a glass atrium in its heart glowing golden, sky bridges and a monorail ring around its middle, a wide plaza at its foot, on a square base of grey deck plates. Grand and luminous, filling most of the base.
