Pump Station: a small drainage pump house. A compact squat pump house of grey metal panels with a slanted roof, a round exhaust vent and a small amber light, a fat suction hose snaking from it into a shallow pool of murky green-blue flood water that covers one half of the square base, an outflow pipe on the other side, the other half of the base already dry clean metal deck plates with a little puddle drying, on a small square plated deck base.
