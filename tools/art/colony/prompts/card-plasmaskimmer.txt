Plasma Skimmer. The square base tile is NOT a metal deck: the whole base is a square pool of glowing swirling magenta and hot pink plasma cloud floating in space, bright magenta light spilling from it, with only a thin dark metal rim around its edge. Hovering low over the middle of this magenta plasma pool: a compact skimmer platform with two magnetic collector pylons with glowing rings drawing a bright stream of magenta plasma up into a white spherical helium tank, cooling fins and teal lights. The magenta plasma base must be clearly visible around the platform.
