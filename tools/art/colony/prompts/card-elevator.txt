Orbital Elevator: a space elevator anchor on the colony's hull. A massive round anchor station of steel and white panels with glowing blue rings, a single thick cable rising straight up and out of the picture, a cargo climber pod with lights halfway up the cable, four heavy stay struts, hazard stripes and red beacons, on a square base of grey hull plating with Earth's blue curve faintly behind. Colossal and awe-inspiring, filling most of the base.
