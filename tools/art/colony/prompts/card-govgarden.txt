Governor's Garden: a grand formal garden around an old white pavilion. Terraced green lawns and clipped hedges, a curved glass glasshouse, a small round lake with a fountain and a footbridge, rows of round flowering trees in pink and white, a domed white pavilion with columns at the top of the terraces, warm lamps along gravel paths, on a square base of rich soil. Peaceful, lush and stately, filling most of the base.
