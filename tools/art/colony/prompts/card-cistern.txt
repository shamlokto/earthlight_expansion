Water Cistern: a great round water storage tank. One large squat cylindrical tank of riveted blue-grey steel with reinforcing bands, a shallow domed lid with a round hatch and a short railing, a vertical glass gauge strip on its side showing clear blue water inside, surrounded by thick looping pipes, valve wheels and a small pump block with blue status lights, on a square base of dark machinery-floor plates with grates. Big and heavy, filling most of the base.
