Geothermal Tap: a heat mine drawing power from the colony's old core. A heavy drilling rig with a short sturdy derrick of dark riveted metal over a round bore hole cut deep into cracked dark rock, the rock around and inside the bore glowing bright molten orange with heat and a faint shimmer, thick insulated heat pipes with copper bands rising from the bore into a squat turbine block with glowing amber vents and cyan status lights, on a square base of dark cracked rock edged with metal plates. Hot and powerful, the orange glow lighting the machinery.
