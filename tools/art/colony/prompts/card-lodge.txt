Wanderers' Lodge: a welcoming travellers' inn. A two-storey building of off-white metal panels and warm wood-brown cladding with a pitched roof and a chimney with a thin wisp of smoke, a covered front porch with warm amber lantern light, a round hanging lantern sign without any writing, a small battered courier hover-bike parked beside the porch, on a square plated deck base. Cosy and inviting.
