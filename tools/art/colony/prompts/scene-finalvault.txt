The Ninefold vault, opened at last, deep inside the colony's sealed core far below the fields: a vast circular vault door made of nine concentric heavy steel rings, rolled open, in a pitch-dark armoured chamber. Warm golden light floods out of the opening onto the dusty floor, and inside, endless shelves of records and ledgers, the registry of Eden's people, dust drifting in the light. Enclosed underground space: no windows, no sky, no Earth, no stars. Solemn, moving, the truth revealed.
