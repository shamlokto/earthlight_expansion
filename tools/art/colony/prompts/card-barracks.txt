Guardian Barracks: a long low two-storey military crew barracks for machine pilots and crews. Olive-grey armoured walls with navy trim, a curved armoured roof, a row of small warm-lit bunk windows, an armoured entrance with a red light and a short rooftop radio mast, a rack of two white pilot helmets and a supply locker by the door, on a square plated deck base. Orderly and military.
