Market Quad: a commercial city block that builds itself up. A square block with market stalls under striped red, yellow and blue awnings at the front, a row of small shopfronts with lit signs behind them, a market hall with a curved glass barrel roof, and in the back corner a slim blue glass trade tower with a glowing gold crown, crates of goods and a few shoppers' lamps, survey stakes with blue flags at the corners, on a square base of pale stone paving. Busy and bright, filling most of the base.
