Coffee Terrace: three stepped green terraces cut into a grassy hillside, each row lined with round dark-green coffee bushes dotted with bright red berries, a small cream-walled roastery with a red tiled roof and a little chimney with a wisp of smoke at the back corner, and at the front a striped yellow café awning over two white round tables. Warm, sunny, inviting, filling most of the square base.
