Mass Driver: a long magnetic launch rail on the colony's hull. A sloped track of glowing blue electromagnetic rings in a steel cradle, angled up toward space, a white cargo pod with an orange stripe sitting at its foot ready to launch, a squat control house with lit windows and a big capacitor bank of copper coils beside it, hazard stripes and a red beacon, on a square base of grey hull plating. Powerful and dramatic, filling most of the base.
