Eden, a vast broken space colony: a huge armoured cylinder-fortress with walled chambers, its hull partly torn open showing green fields, a river and lit decks inside, scaffolding and repair lights on the damaged sections. It hangs just above Earth, whose great curved blue limb and thin glowing atmosphere fill the bottom of the picture. Behind it drifting asteroid belts, a faint violet plasma cloud and stars. Near the colony's dock a small fleet of worn warships and shuttles with tiny dock lights is moored. Dawn sunlight breaking over Earth's horizon and catching the colony: hopeful, after the war, the start of rebuilding.
