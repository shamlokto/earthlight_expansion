The reference picture is the game's title illustration: keep its painting style, colours, lighting and the same colony and the same Earth, as a later moment of the same scene.

Earthlight: seen from inside Eden through a great curved sky panel, colonists stand silhouetted at the glass looking down at the night side of Earth below, mostly dark and brown, and on its face one bright patch of green and blue along a coastline, with tiny lights of domes in it: the Hope, the first green on Earth in a generation. A white generation ship crosses the view toward it on a thin blue trail. Quiet, tearful, hopeful: Earth's recovery has begun.
