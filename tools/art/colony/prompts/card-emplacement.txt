Battery Emplacement: a heavy defensive gun turret mounted on the colony's outer hull. A low wide angular armoured turret housing in gunmetal and off-white plates on a round rotating ring mount, two long heavy parallel cannon barrels angled up toward space with cyan-glowing coil rings along them, a small boxy missile launcher pod with six round tubes on the turret's side, a red sensor eye on the front, on a square base tile of riveted grey outer-hull armour plating with panel seams and yellow-and-black hazard stripes around the ring. An original turret design, heavy and menacing.
