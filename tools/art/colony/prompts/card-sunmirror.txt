Sun Mirror: a great round concave foil mirror, silver and faceted like a dish of many hexagonal panels, tilted on a slim gold truss frame, focusing a bright golden beam onto a small boiler pod held on an arm in front of it, a squat power module with radiator fins at its foot, floating in space on a small square platform of dark hull plating, stars faint around it. Bright and elegant, filling most of the base.
