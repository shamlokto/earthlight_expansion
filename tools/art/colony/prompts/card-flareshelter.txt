Flare Shelter: a low armoured bunker. A squat half-buried shelter with thick sloped walls of dark grey shielding, a heavy round blast door with yellow and black hazard stripes, a small glowing orange warning light, a row of heavy cable conduits running into a shielded switchyard with chunky transformers, on a square base of grey deck plating. Sturdy and reassuring, filling most of the base.
