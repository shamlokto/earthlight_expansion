Gallery: an elegant concert and exhibition hall. A graceful long building of off-white panels with a row of tall rounded arches along its front, soft warm golden light glowing from inside the arches, slim columns with gold trim, a gently curved barrel-vault roof of pale panels with a narrow strip of skylight glass, a short flight of wide steps up to the entrance, two small ornamental lamp posts, on a square plated deck base. Refined and calm, the most elegant building in the colony.
