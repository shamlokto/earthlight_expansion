Beyond Eden, long after Eden was restored: the same vast cylinder colony above the same Earth, whole and glowing gold along its glass bands, with a busy starport of lit piers beside its hull. Leaving it, in the foreground and climbing away toward the stars, a long gleaming white generation ship with a wide ring of lit windows around its middle and three engine bells burning a bright blue-white trail, small escort ships peeling away from it with warm engine lights. Earth below is bright blue with white clouds; deep space ahead is full of stars, one of them brighter than the rest. Quiet, awe-struck, hopeful: a new beginning.
