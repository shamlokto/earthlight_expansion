The Ark, a generation ship in its dock: a long gleaming white cylindrical starship lying in a cradle of yellow construction gantries, ringed by bands of lit windows, a wide habitat ring with a gold band around its middle, a pointed nose at one end and three big engine bells glowing blue at the other, on a square of dark hull plating edged with a glowing teal ring and red corner beacons. Epic, hopeful and enormous, the greatest thing the colony ever built, filling most of the square base.
