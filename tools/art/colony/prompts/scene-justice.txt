Justice for Eden: inside the colony's Hall of Names, a long quiet hall of warm lamps hung in rows, its walls lined floor to ceiling with engraved name plates and open ledgers of the registry. A small crowd of colonists in work clothes stands and listens, heads bowed, while an older woman warden in a dark coat reads aloud from a great open book at a lectern. Behind them a tall window looks out on deep space, where the broken wreck of a large dark battleship drifts away into the stars, small and far off, trailing faint debris. Warm golden lamplight inside, cold blue starlight outside. Solemn, dignified, quietly triumphant: the names remembered, the guilty brought to answer. No text, no readable writing.
