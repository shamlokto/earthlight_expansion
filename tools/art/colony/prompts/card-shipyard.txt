Shipyard: an orbital construction slip built on the colony's outer hull. A long open lattice gantry frame of grey girders cradling the half-built grey hull of a small warship (its bow finished, its stern still bare ribs), two tall yellow gantry cranes, tiny welding sparks and amber work lights, on a large square base tile of riveted grey outer-hull armour plating with panel seams and a few yellow-and-black hazard stripes.
