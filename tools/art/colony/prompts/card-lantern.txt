The Lantern: a tall slender tower of light. A stepped white stone plinth with gold trim, a tall tapering white tower with glowing window bands, crowned with a huge faceted glass lantern blazing warm golden light and sending a beam straight up into the dark, four smaller lamp posts at the corners, on a square base of pale paving. Hopeful, monumental and luminous, the brightest thing in the picture, filling most of the base.
