Ruess's Workshop: a great engineering hall, the professor's own works. A long brick-and-steel hall with a sawtooth glass roof glowing warm inside, a tall yellow gantry crane over an open yard with a half-built machine on trestles, stacks of steel beams and crates, a drafting tower with lit windows and a big round workshop clock, sparks from a welding bay, on a square base of grey deck plating. Busy, proud and grand, filling most of the base.
