Living Quad: a residential city block that builds itself up. A square block divided into four lots at different stages of growth: a little cottage with a red roof and a garden, a row of two-storey terraced houses with coloured doors, a cream courtyard block of flats with a green roof garden, and in the back corner a tall slim residential tower of warm lit windows and balconies, small round trees and a lawn between them, survey stakes with green flags at the corners, on a square base of grey deck plates. Homely and growing, filling most of the base.
