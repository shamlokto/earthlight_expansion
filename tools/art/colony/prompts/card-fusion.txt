Fusion Reactor: a large fusion power plant. A big doughnut-shaped torus reactor ring held in a heavy open armoured frame, glowing hot white and cyan plasma visible through windows around the ring, thick magnet coils clamped around it, two round helium-3 fuel tanks with gold bands feeding it through pipes, a squat control block with lit windows, on a large square plated deck base. Powerful and bright, the glow lighting its surroundings.
