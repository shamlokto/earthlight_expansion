The reference picture is the game's title illustration: keep its painting style, colours, lighting and the same colony and the same Earth, as a later moment of the same scene.

Eden Restored, years after the title picture: the same vast cylinder colony above the same Earth, now whole. Its hull is fully repaired with clean off-white and gunmetal panels, no scaffolding and no tears; through long lit windows and glass bands along its length the three decks glow warm gold, and green fields, woods and a bright river are visible inside. Every dock is busy: a long line of relief convoy ships with small warm engine lights streams from the colony down toward Earth, curving into Earth's glowing atmosphere. Earth below is bright and blue with white clouds, its day side lit by a golden sunrise breaking over the horizon and catching the colony. Calm, triumphant, hopeful: the end of the story.
