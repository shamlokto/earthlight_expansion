Jammer Array: no building body at all, only an open cluster of antennae. A low flat square dark gunmetal equipment plinth, barely knee-high, from which rise a dense forest of thin black and dark-grey spiky antenna rods of many different heights, some bent at angles like a bristling crown of spikes, with three small angled emitter dishes among them; faint violet interference ripples and static rings radiating from the rod tips, a few tiny red status lights. All dark metal, no white panels, no tanks, no dome. Standing on a small square base tile that is a dark navy space tile with a few faint stars, framed by a thin metal rim. Stealthy, spiky and dark.
