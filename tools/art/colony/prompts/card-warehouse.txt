Warehouse: a long, wide general storage hall of ribbed gunmetal and pale grey steel with a low curved barrel roof, three big roll-up loading doors along its front with yellow-black hazard edging, stacks of colourful cargo crates, barrels and blue water drums beside the doors, a small forklift, a row of amber lamps along the roofline, on a square base of deck plates. Broad and solid, filling most of the base.
