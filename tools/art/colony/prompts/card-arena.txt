Zero-G Arena: a great round stepped bowl of grey and silver stands with a glowing teal rim and colourful awnings, a bright green lit field in the middle, and floating above the field a huge translucent glowing blue sphere with tiny gold players flying inside it; four tall floodlight masts with blazing lamps at the corners, on a dark metal deck base. Exciting, spectacular, a stadium of the future, filling most of the square base.
