Workshop Quad: an industrial city block that builds itself up. A square block with two small grey workshop sheds with sawtooth roofs and skylights at the front, a yard of wooden crates and a little yellow crane, a bigger machine hall behind, and in the back corner a factory with tall chimney stacks glowing orange at the top and a round white tank, survey stakes with orange flags at the corners, on a square base of dark grey deck plates. Hard-working and warm, filling most of the base.
