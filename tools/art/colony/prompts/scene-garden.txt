The Garden of Eden, long after Eden was restored: the same vast cylinder colony above the same Earth, its glass bands now showing deep green inside, forests, orchards and terraced sky gardens glowing in soft golden light, a silver river winding through meadows. Earth below is bright blue with white clouds; deep space is full of stars. Peaceful, lush, alive: a garden in the sky.
