The Hope, Earth's first new colony: low over a ruined, poisoned coastline under a yellow-brown sky, a long gleaming white generation ship with a ring of lit windows descends on blue engine light toward a small cleared landing field ringed with amber lights. Below: broken city ruins, pools of yellow-green toxic gas, a black poisoned sea along the shore, a violet glow from an old crater. A few tiny suited figures and three pale pressure tents wait on the field. Bleak, enormous and brave: the beginning of Earth's recovery.
