Ice Harvester: a sturdy mining rig with a rotating cutter wheel biting into ice, a heated melting tank with a plume of white steam, insulated pipes and a round water tank with blue lights. Its base is not a metal deck but a square slab of pale blue-white comet ice floating in space: glittering frost, cyan ice crystals and frozen chunks, with a faint glowing trail of icy dust streaming off one side.
