Grand Arboretum: a vast forest under glass. A huge ribbed glass dome on a round white stone drum with a glowing teal band, and inside it one enormous ancient tree with a broad green crown dotted with pink blossom; stepped green garden terraces around the dome edged with gold, narrow blue canals glowing along the edges, two smaller round glasshouses at the corners and an avenue of flowering trees, on a wide square green base. Serene, lush and monumental, the greenest thing in the picture, filling most of the base.
