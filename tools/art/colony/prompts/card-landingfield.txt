The Landing Field of the Hope, Earth: a square sealed landing pad of grey plating on dead brown ash ground, a glowing amber ring of landing lights and red corner beacons, three small pale pressure tents and a squat stores block with an amber light, a thin mast with an orange windsock, yellow-brown toxic haze at the edges of the base. Bleak, brave, the first foothold on a poisoned Earth, filling most of the square base.
