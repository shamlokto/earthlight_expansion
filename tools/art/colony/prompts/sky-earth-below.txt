Earth seen from low orbit: the curved planet fills the whole lower half of the picture, deep blue oceans with a sickly teal-green tint and swirls of white cloud, the horizon a gentle wide arc across the middle of the picture with a thin bright cyan-blue atmosphere glow along it.
