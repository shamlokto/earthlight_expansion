16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single "Naga" heavy gun pod: a squat armoured hover platform with no legs, a boxy sloped armoured body with a narrow glowing cyan visor slit, a stack of three long parallel railgun rails on top pointing forward to the lower left, and underneath them a thick ringed beam cannon barrel with a glowing cyan emitter at the muzzle, fed by round blue capacitor orbs on its flank, a hover skid with small thrusters underneath. Three-quarter view, guns pointing to the lower left. Gunmetal green and dark olive armour plates, black panel lines, cyan and blue lights, a little dull gold trim. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole machine is centred and fills about 80% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted slate-grey background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
