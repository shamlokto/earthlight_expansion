16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single heroic flagship, the "Azoras": a heavy carrier-battlecruiser built around a thick armoured cylindrical drum hull lined with rows of small gold turrets, drawn out at the front into a long sharp needle prow, with two forward-swept wings ending in big wingtip engine pods, and a massive twin-barrelled heavy cannon mounted on each wing. A heavy three-nozzle engine block at the stern with glowing exhaust. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Pearl-white armour with gold leading edges and a bright pink stripe along the hull, cyan glowing lights. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
