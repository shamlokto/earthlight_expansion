== lobster (picture; style reference: js/art/pallas.js picture)
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single sleek space interceptor fighter, the "Lobster Kai": a refit of a lobster-like interceptor with two long forward twin pulse-beam cannon prongs like claws, swept wings, a small missile rack under each wing, big glowing engine at the rear. Three-quarter view, nose pointing to the lower left. White armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan canopy. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole craft is centred and fills about 80% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.

== titan (picture; style reference: js/art/dirtalos.js picture)
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single sturdy humanoid combat mecha, the "Titan Kai": a heavy-set real-robot suit with broad armoured shoulders, a visor head, holding a very long railgun sniper rifle in both hands aimed to the lower left, a compact twin-barrel anti-ship cannon mounted on its right shoulder, thruster pack on its back. Full body, three-quarter view, dynamic firing stance. White armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan visor and sensor lights. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole mecha is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
