== robot-src.jpg (reference: the Titan Kai picture tools/art/kai/titan-src.jpg, for the Kai style)
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, in the same style as the reference picture. A single slim agile transforming humanoid mecha, the "Athena Kai" hunter form: real-robot proportions, a compact head with twin swept-back antennae and a cyan visor (no V crest), a chest made from a folded jet nose with a cyan canopy, shoulder armour made from jet air intakes, tall twin tail fins folded on its back like a backpack. It holds a long railgun rifle in both hands, aimed to the lower left, and the rifle carries a glowing hot-pink energy bayonet blade under its muzzle. Full body, three-quarter view, dynamic stance facing the lower left. White armour with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan glass. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. Centred, filling about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.

== jet-src.jpg (reference: robot-src.jpg)
16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game, in the same style as the reference picture. The same machine as the reference, transformed into its jet form, the "Athena Kai" fighter-bomber: a sleek space fighter with a long pointed nose and cyan bubble canopy, swept-back wings with gold wingtips, twin tall tail fins, side air intakes, the long railgun mounted along the belly, and one large heavy torpedo slung under the fuselage. Three-quarter view, nose pointing to the lower left. White armour with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan glass, exactly the reference's colours. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. Centred, filling about 80% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.

== spent-src.jpg (reference: jet-src.jpg)
The exact same fighter jet, same view, same colours, same pixel-art style and the same plain background, but the large dark torpedo under the fuselage is gone (it has been launched): nothing hangs under the belly except the long railgun. Nothing else changes.

== jet-side.jpg (reference: jet-src.jpg)
Pixel-art game sprite of this same fighter jet with its torpedo, in strict side profile, nose facing right, flying level. Same white, magenta-pink and gold colours, cyan canopy, same design: long pointed nose, swept wings with gold tips, twin tall tail fins, side intake, the railgun along the belly and the large dark torpedo under it. Chunky readable silhouette, strong dark outline, high-contrast cel shading. No engine flame. Centred, filling most of the frame width. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no text, no border, no shadow.
