16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single colossal ancient-imperial space fortress-ship, the "Nineveh", sister of a legendary mobile fortress: an immense layered hull like a flying citadel, obsidian and deep emerald-green armour plates edged with ornate burnished gold, tiered gold-trimmed towers and battlements along its back, rows of gun turrets and missile silos, a crown-like command spire, huge engines glowing gold-white at the stern, and at the bow an enormous main cannon barrel with a blazing white-gold sun of energy at its muzzle. Majestic, ominous, grand. Three-quarter view from above, bow pointing to the lower left, stern to the upper right. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted dark slate-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
