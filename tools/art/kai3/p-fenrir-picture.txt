16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single sleek fast space destroyer, the "Fenrir Kai": a long narrow wolf-like hull with a sharp angular prow, one very long heavy railgun barrel running along its spine and projecting far beyond the prow, a small armoured bridge, two short swept fins, a compact missile pod on each flank, and two big glowing engines at the stern. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Pearl-white armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan glowing lights. Same art style and colour scheme as the reference, but a different, smaller and slimmer ship. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
