16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single small, lean, aggressive space GUNBOAT built for hit-and-run raids, the "Fenrir Kai": a low flat wedge-shaped hull like a raider, dominated by ONE oversized heavy railgun mounted along its spine that is almost as long as the whole boat and projects far beyond the sharp prow, two huge engine nacelles at the stern glowing hot orange, small angled fins, a tiny armoured cockpit. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Colours: dark teal and sea-green armour plates, white hull numbering "71" stencil, bright orange-red warning stripes and accents, gunmetal grey railgun, orange engine glow. No pink, no magenta. Same pixel-art style as the reference, but a different ship. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text other than the hull number, no border, no shadows on the background.
