16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single GIGANTIC GUN BATTLESHIP, the "Asura-II Kai": a massive, heavy, armoured warship whose guns are the whole point: three huge triple-barrel main gun turrets lined up along its long armoured deck, each barrel enormous and long, plus rows of smaller secondary gun turrets along both flanks, a tall stepped command tower, thick armour belts, four big engines at the stern. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Colours: deep navy blue and steel-blue armour, polished gold trim lines, dark gunmetal barrels with red-hot glowing muzzles, small cyan bridge windows. No pink, no magenta, no white hull. Same pixel-art style as the reference, but a different, much bigger and heavier ship. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 90% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
