16-bit Super Famicom era pixel art unit portrait for a 1992 Japanese space strategy game. A single heavy space battleship, the "Asura-II Kai": a broad armoured wedge-shaped hull with a raised command tower, a triple rail-cannon battery of long barrels on a turret at the front, rows of missile tubes along both armoured flanks, side sponsons with smaller guns, and a wide block of four glowing engines at the stern. Three-quarter view from above, prow pointing to the lower left. Pearl-white armour plates with magenta-pink stripes, gold trim, cyan glowing lights. Same art style and colour scheme as the reference, but a different, heavier and broader ship. Clean dark outline, cel shading with 4-5 tones per colour, crisp limited palette. The whole ship is centred and fills about 85% of the frame. Plain flat solid muted lavender-blue background, no stars, no text, no border, no shadows on the background.
