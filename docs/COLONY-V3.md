# Earthlight Space Colony Rebuild — version 3 ("Guardians") build contract

Owner, 2026-11-03: "ready for more buildings, techs and raids"; "production for Earth Light units as
well, just like the expansion"; "credits and parts can build ship/units". Builds on COLONY-V1.md and
COLONY-V2.md (still authoritative for what isn't changed). The owner has already unlocked all of
version 2 in their save: version 3 must load old saves and simply offer the new techs and buildings.
Worker classes and schools are deferred (not in this version). Manual hex battles are version 4.

## Data (written; the sim owns the numbers)
* `data/buildings.js`: 13 new buildings (Arsenal, Guardian Barracks, Scrapyard and Battery Emplacement
  at 80 colonists; Shipyard, Sensor Mast, Shield Pylon, Jammer Array, Fusion Reactor, Mushroom Wall,
  Terrace Homes, Wanderers' Lodge, Officers' Quarters by research); category `defence`; fields `builds`, `crews`.
* `data/tech.js`: 11 new techs (30 in all), branch `defence`; effect `{ kind: 'machines', tier }`.
* `data/machines.js` (generated by `node tools/colony-machines.mjs` from the expansion's units.json):
  50 machines `{ type, name, code, cls, category, hp, move, evasion, attack, defence, price, at, tier,
  series, unique }`; `MACHINE_COST(m)` = `{ credits: price × 10, parts: price }` (owner: credits and
  parts build units). Tier 1 is available with the Arsenal; tiers 2, 3, 4 need Machine Works, Heavy
  Works, Kai Refits. `at: 'shipyard'` machines need a finished Shipyard. `unique` machines: one at a time
  (the expansion's rule).
* `data/commanders.js`: `GENERAL`, `STARTERS` (Sato), 12 `WANDERERS`, `GRADE`, `STYLES`, `PERKS`,
  `DEPARTMENTS`, `BASE_COMMANDERS` (2), `PER_QUARTERS` (2).

## State additions (state.js; defaults for old saves in upgradeState)
```js
state.guardians: [{ id, type, level, hp, commander: null | key, free: daysOfFreeUpkeep }]
    // existing beta guardians gain hp = full and free = 60 (D7); new machines level 0, free 0
state.production: [{ id, type, at: buildingId, days }]   // one order per Arsenal / Shipyard at a time; a queue behind it
state.commanders: [{ key, loyalty, injured: days, dept: null | deptKey }]   // starts: general, sato
state.wanderers: [{ key, until: day, asked: n }]          // at the Lodge now
state.raid: { heat, next: day, warned: false, coming: null | Raid, last: null | Report, count, won, lost }
    Raid = { day, from: 'north' | 'east' | 'west' | 'earth', tier, units: [{ type, level }], faction, strength }
    Report = { day, won, from, faction, lostGuardians: [types], damaged: [buildingIds], stolen: { good: n }, salvage: { parts, credits }, tribute }
buildings[i].damaged: days left out of action (raid damage; repaired by a crew like construction)
```

## Rules (rules.js), new
* Production: `machineList(state)` → `[{ ...machine, cost, ok, why }]` (why: "Research Machine Works.",
  "Build a Shipyard.", "Only one Azoras Kai may serve at a time.", afford reasons);
  `orderMachine(state, type)` (pays `costOf(MACHINE_COST)` at once; queues at the least busy Arsenal or
  Shipyard; days = max(2, ceil(price / 4))); `cancelOrder(state, id)` (full refund).
* Guardians: `crewCap(state)` (4 from the Command Centre + 6 per Barracks), `sellGuardian` (exists;
  +20% with a finished Scrapyard), `machineUpkeep(state)` (each guardian: ceil(price / 8) credits a
  day; free while `free > 0`; Quartermaster perk halves it), repairs (a Scrapyard repairs 2 HP a day
  per damaged guardian at 1 part per HP).
* Commanders: `commanderCap(state)`, `assignCommander(state, key, guardianId | null)`,
  `setDepartment(state, key, dept | null)` (one head per department), `inviteWanderer(state, key)`
  (gift 100 credits; chance = 0.3 + general.charm / 220 − 0.03 × (commanders − 2) + (100 − loyalty) / 400,
  clamped 0.05..0.95; a refusal lowers the wanderer's loyalty by 8 so the next try is likelier),
  `dismissCommander(state, key)` (they become a wanderer again later).
* Commander strength (`guardianPower(state, g)`): base = (attack + defence) × hp / maxHp × (1 + 0.08 ×
  level); the General: × GENERAL_POWER × (1 + (GENERAL_EVASION − 1) / 2) = ×3; another commander:
  × (1 + (GRADE[grade of the machine's class] − 1) × might / 100), never above ×1.6 power; style +15%
  when it matches the machine's main weapon mode; perk effects. A guardian without a crew doesn't fight.
* Raids: `raidPreview(state)` → `{ winChance, defence, attack, expectedLost: [types], expectedDamage,
  tribute: { credits, food } }` for the coming raid; `answerRaid(state, 'fight' | 'tribute')`
  (also reachable through `answer()` of the raid's pending event).
* `techEffect(state, 'machines')` → the highest tier unlocked (1 with no tech).

## Simulation (sim.js), new
* **Heat** (0..100, eased 10% a day toward its target): pop / 20 + stored value / 2000 + open chambers
  × 2 + sales over 30 days / 1000 − 10 per Jammer (max −30); Cass Rook serving +5.
* **Raid clock**: none before 80 colonists and before day 60 (Easy) / 35 (Normal). Then the next raid
  comes in 30–45 days (Easy) / 20–35 (Normal), sooner with heat (× (1.4 − heat / 100)). A warning
  pending event (kind `raidwarn`, speaker `sato`, says from where, what kind, how many days, and that
  Arsenal + Barracks + Battery Emplacements defend) comes 3 days ahead, +3 per Sensor Mast (max +9).
* **Raid strength** by tier, from day and heat: scavengers (1–3 tier-1 machines, level 0), pirates
  (4–8, tiers 1–2, levels 0–2), warbands (8–14, tiers 1–3, levels 2–4), remnant (15+, a capital ship,
  levels 4–6). Easy: strength × 0.6. Faction: scavengers, the Ashfall Cartel (pirates; more often if
  `imported.choice === 'refuse'`), Union and Galt deserters (warbands), the Directorate remnant.
* **Arrival**: a pending event kind `raid` (speaker `sato`) with choices `fight` and `tribute`; its
  text includes `raidPreview` (win chance, likely losses, tribute price). It pauses the clock.
* **Fight (auto-resolve)**: defence = Σ guardianPower over crewed, undamaged guardians + 40 per Battery
  Emplacement (Dragon ×1.3 against ships, Hydra ×1.3 against small craft) × Security head bonus;
  attack = Σ the raiders' power. Win chance = d² / (d² + a²). Roll with the seeded rng. Guardians take
  damage (HP) proportional to a / (a + d), more when losing; a guardian at 0 HP is destroyed; its
  commander is injured 20–40 days. Survivors gain levels (+1, +2 for the commander's machine).
  **Lost**: 10–30% of food, parts, alloy and credits stolen; 1–4 buildings within 30 tiles of the
  raiders' side damaged for 5–15 days unless a Shield Pylon covers them; fear −15 happiness decaying
  over 15 days. **Won**: salvage = raiders' prices × 5 credits and × 0.5 parts (Hunter +20%); fear −5.
* **Tribute**: credits = raid strength × 3 (or food if credits are short); heat +10; −5 happiness for 15 days.
* **Production** advances each day at Arsenals / Shipyards that are working (staffed, powered).
* **Wanderers**: with a Wanderers' Lodge, one arrives every 15 days (Inn 10, Grand Lodge 7), stays 20
  days, from the WANDERERS not serving or present; a pending event (kind `wanderer`) announces each.
* **Commanders**: wages paid daily (unpaid → loyalty −2 a day); loyalty drifts toward 40 + happiness / 2;
  below 20 they may leave (Steady never does); injured ones recover. Department heads apply their bonus.
* Fusion Reactor uses 2 helium a day (overdrive 4, +50% power, 1% a day of a 5-day shutdown); without
  helium it makes no power. Mushroom Walls need no water. Terrace Homes are housing 60 with `service`.
* Beta: imported guardians start with `free: 60` (no upkeep for 60 days, D7). Guardians eat no food (D14).

## Goals (goals.js, the lead): after Apartments: an Arsenal, a first machine, a Barracks, survive a raid.

## View (view3d/)
Kits for the 13 buildings (the Shipyard with a hull-mounted slip, the Arsenal with a hangar door, the
Emplacement as a Dragon/Hydra-style turret by mode, Sensor Mast dish, Shield Pylon with a dome
shimmer, Jammer with antennae, Fusion Reactor torus glowing, Mushroom Wall, Terrace Homes on the
hill, Lodge, Quarters). **Guardians on the map**: a few guardians idle beside the Arsenal / dock
(small mecha and ships by class). **Raid animation** when `state.raid.last.day === state.day` or a
raid is `coming` within a day: raiders fly in from `from` (map edge or up from the atmosphere),
guardians lift off to meet them, beam and missile flashes, explosions on losses; a damaged building
smokes while `damaged > 0`. Visual only.

## Interface
A **Defence** panel (top-bar button and key G) with tabs: **Guardians** (list: machine, class, level,
HP bar, commander, upkeep; sell, repair status), **Production** (the machine list by class like the
expansion's production list: name, code, HP, move, attack / defence, price in credits and parts,
tier lock reasons; Order; the queue with days left and Cancel), **Commanders** (the commanders with
stats, grades, style, perk, loyalty, wage, injured; assign to a guardian; head a department;
wanderers at the Lodge with Invite and the chance), **Raids** (heat meter, next raid ETA once warned,
last raid report). A **raid modal** for the `raid` event: from where, who, the preview (win chance as
a bar, likely losses, tribute price), buttons Fight / Pay tribute. The top bar gets a small heat /
raid indicator once raids are possible. How to play: a Defence section. The build menu's Defence tab.
